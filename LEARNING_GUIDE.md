# Learning guide

Before presenting this repository, be able to explain sampling frequency and Nyquist frequency; DC offset; band-pass and notch filtering; zero-phase filtering; rectification; RMS envelopes; Welch power spectral density; and why this workflow is not motor-unit decomposition.

## Exercises
1. Compare RMS windows of 50, 100 and 250 ms.
2. Change the lower band-pass cutoff and explain the effect.
3. Add one analysis of your own.
4. Write a short limitations paragraph in your own words.

Run: `python generate_synthetic_data.py` then `python analysis.py`.
